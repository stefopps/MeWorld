That pink sliver is alpha, made visible. It's not a separate number you set and forget — it's the actual area under the null curve (gray) that sits past the red dashed line. That area is exactly the chance of a false alarm: the null is true, nothing's really happening, and by pure luck the data still lands past the threshold. Drag alpha down and the sliver shrinks — you're demanding stronger evidence before shouting "real effect." Drag it up and the sliver grows — easier to declare victory, but more of those victories will be flukes.
