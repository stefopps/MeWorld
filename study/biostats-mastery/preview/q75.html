Key insight: The patient is a single diagnosed case asking about her own outcome — that is a proportion (CFR), not a comparison between two groups (AR, RR) and not about treatment effect (NNT, NNH). Every epidemiological statistic answers a specific, narrow question. Using the wrong one gives the right answer to the wrong question.
