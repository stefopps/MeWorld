The CI significance test on a ratio scale: If the 95% CI includes 1.0 (the null value for RR/OR/HR), the result is NOT statistically significant. If the entire CI is above 1.0 → significant positive association. If the entire CI is below 1.0 → significant protective effect.
This CI [0.98, 1.18] crosses 1.0 — the data are compatible with an RR of 1 (no effect), a protective effect (RR 0.98), or an increase (RR 1.18). Not enough evidence to reject the null.
