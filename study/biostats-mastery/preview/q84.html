Question 84 · Normal Distribution — Empiric Rule & Test-Positive Region

A new biomarker, PD-1, is normally distributed with μ = 100 mg/dL, σ = 20 mg/dL. Patients with PD-1 below 60 mg/dL are test-positive. What percentage of the population would test positive?

Cutoff at 60 = μ − 2σ. Drag the cutoff slider to move the knife across the distribution — the red tail area is your test-positive %. Toggle σ-bands ON to see the empiric rule layers (68% / 95% / 99.7%). An α-knife arrow descends to the cutoff, and a connector shows that the 2.5% tail = (100% − 95%) ÷ 2 — proving where the answer comes from.

The empiric rule: 68% within ±1σ, 95% within ±2σ, 99.7% within ±3σ. At μ−2σ, the left tail = (100% − 95%) / 2 = 2.5%.
