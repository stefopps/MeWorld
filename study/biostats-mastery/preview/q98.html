Which features of a test depend on the prevalence of the disease in the population?

The rule: PPV and NPV are prevalence-dependent — they change with the population you test. Sensitivity and specificity are inherent test properties — they stay constant regardless of disease prevalence. A negative mammogram is more reassuring in a screening population (low prevalence, high NPV) than in a high-risk diagnostic clinic.
