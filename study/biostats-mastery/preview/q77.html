The practical CI interpretation: "If we repeated this study 100 times with fresh samples, about 95 of those CIs would capture the true OR." Most exam boards accept "95% chance the true OR is between 3.2 and 7.8" as the MC answer because it matches the Bayesian intuition clinicians actually use — even though strict frequentists say the CI describes the procedure, not a probability on any one interval.
The null line (OR = 1) is far to the left — the CI doesn't touch it, so the association is statistically significant.
