Which biomarker is most useful to confirm the diagnosis of CHF?

Scenario: Five candidate biomarkers for CHF in low-resource settings. "Confirm" means rule IN — you need the test with the highest specificity (fewest false positives). The PPV curve shows, for every prevalence level, how often a positive result is actually correct. Drag the prevalence slider to see which biomarker stays highest — that's your confirmatory test.

The rule: Screening tests need high sensitivity (rule OUT — SnNout). Confirmatory tests need high specificity (rule IN — SpPin).
CHF5 has Sp=93% — at any prevalence, its PPV curve sits above all others. CHF2 (Sn=95%, Sp=56%) is a screening profile: great for ruling OUT, terrible for confirming.
