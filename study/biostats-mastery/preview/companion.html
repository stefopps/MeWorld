Micro story: ROC measures inherent discrimination — how well a test separates diseased from healthy, independent of prevalence. Each biomarker is one point. The diagonal = random guessing. Upper-left = best. Notice CHF2 (high Sn) is near the upper-left despite having low PPV on the other tab — different questions, different tools.
