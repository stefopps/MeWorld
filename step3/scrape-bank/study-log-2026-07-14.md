# Study Log — 2026-07-14

## Set 6 — ARDS
- **Priming mechanism**: Pancreatic inflammation already primes the immune system — threshold of immune cells is raised. Any insult (e.g. pneumonia) triggers a rapid flare.
- **Alveolar collapse**: Arterial walls are digested → fluid moves from vessel into alveoli → washes out surfactant → alveolar collapse. Dead alveoli = no gas exchange = diffuse gray opacifications on imaging.
- **P/F ratio (PaO2 / FiO2)**: PaO2 = oxygen tension in blood. FiO2 = fraction of inspired oxygen (room air = 21%). Expected ratio if all 21% oxygen enters blood ≈ 100/0.21 ≈ 476. Ratio < 300 = underoxygenated (ARDS threshold). Expect lactic acid to skyrocket.

## Set 14 — Nutritional Determinants of Health
- **TB reactivation without HIV**: Malnutrition itself is immunocompromise. Antibodies are made of protein. Catabolic state → stress compromises immune system. CD4 ~540, TB reactivated, but no HIV — hunger as immunosuppression.
- **Ultra-processed foods**: Technically poisons — mess up blood pressure and quality of life. Ulcers → amputations, diabetes, prediabetes. Excess sodium → hypertension.
- **Alcohol — fetal alcohol spectrum**: Alcohol blocks neural crest cell migration from source to body. Reduced cell count → microcephaly, sacral/caudal agenesis, missing feet, flat buttocks. Brain tissue literally has fewer cells.

## Set 104 — Breast Cancer / Desmoplasia & Vaginal Physiology
- **Desmoplastic reaction**: Cancer cells trigger fibrotic extensions that pull on the dermis → inward retraction of skin/nipple. Bleeding nipple as presenting sign.
- **Vaginal lubrication cascade** (postmenopausal dryness context):
  1. Vaginal epithelial cells contain glycogen
  2. Lactobacillus ferments glycogen → lactic acid → low pH → protection
  3. Arousal → vasocongestion → increased hydrostatic pressure → transudation of fluid → lubrication
  4. Entire process is estrogen-dependent. Postmenopausal low estrogen → cascade doesn't trigger → dryness
- **Lichen sclerosus** and **systemic sclerosis** also in the differential for these tissues.

## Set 20 — Biochemistry & CT Evolution
- **B6 (pyridoxal phosphate)**:
  - Amino acid metabolism: transamination (AST/ALT), cystathionine → cysteine, methionine pathways
  - Neurochemistry: DOPA pathway, neurotransmitter synthesis
  - Found throughout: amino acid metabolism, neurotransmitter synthesis, heme synthesis (ALA synthase)
- **B12**: Entry point into cytosol, involved in heme synthesis, requires intrinsic factor.
- **CT density evolution**: Blood clot starts hyperdense (~20-30 Hounsfield units). Over time, plasmin cleaves fibrin → clot dissolves → becomes isodense with brain tissue. "Inside vs outside" timing distinction.
