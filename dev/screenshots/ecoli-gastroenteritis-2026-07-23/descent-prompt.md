Enterotoxigenic E. coli colonizes the small intestine, releasing enterotoxins that activate cAMP → CFTR chloride channels → chloride pours into the lumen → water follows by osmosis → secretory diarrhea despite an intact epithelium. Dehydration worsens until stool culture confirms E. coli sensitive to ciprofloxacin. Cipro binds DNA gyrase, blocking replication and killing the bacteria while IV fluids restore volume.
