## What Was Missed

| Missed Order | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Calprotectin, stool** | Mucosal protein. ESR alone is insufficient — calprotectin + ESR together lock non-inflammatory. |
| **CRP** | Independent acute-phase reactant, different from ESR. Always pair with ESR in acute diarrhea. |
| **Hepatitis A IgM** | HAV can present with diarrhea + abdominal pain. Standard acute diarrhea panel. |
| **Smoking cessation counseling** | Free point. Every CCS smoker needs this. |
| **Medication compliance counseling** | Antibiotics in a smoker — adherence is a scoring item. |

---

## Timing Errors

- **Tdap** ordered too early — placed before the acute treatment was resolved. Preventive care belongs at the end.
- **One negative patient update** — patient worsened (vomiting, dizzy) on Day 3 before IV cipro was started. With culture results at Day 2, treatment should have been immediate.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Non-inflammatory vs inflammatory diarrhea** — ESR and calprotectin together are the key discriminators. Blood in stool, fever, and leukocytosis = inflammatory. Normal labs, no blood, no fever = secretory.
2. **Stool culture sensitivity-guided antibiotics** — E. coli in this case was cephalosporin-resistant; ciprofloxacin was the treatment. Don't blanket-cover with a cephalosporin without sensitivities.
3. **Giardia negative changed the plan** — metronidazole was unnecessary once Giardia antigen came back negative. Titrate treatment to results.
4. **Preventive care timing** — Tdap and smoking cessation belong at the *end* of the case, not in the diagnostic phase.
