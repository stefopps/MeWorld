3x3 grid, 9 panels — what was missed in a ruptured ectopic pregnancy case: diagnostics, treatment, prevention. The patient was diagnosed correctly but never went to surgery. The case timed out.

Camera: medium, medium, close, medium, wide, medium, medium, medium, wide.

Panel 1 (Transvaginal US — DIAGNOSIS): ER bay. Woman on gurney. Transvaginal ultrasound probe being positioned. Screen visible: empty uterus + adnexal mass + free fluid in pelvic cul-de-sac. The gold standard for ectopic. Empty uterus + positive β-hCG means ectopic until proven otherwise. Transabdominal ultrasound may miss it — TVUS is definitive.

Panel 2 (Pre-op EKG — SAFETY): EKG leads on patient's chest. 12-lead tracing printing — normal sinus rhythm. Pre-operative clearance. Every surgical patient gets an EKG before anesthesia. She's tachycardic from blood loss, not arrhythmia — good to confirm.

Panel 3 (Type and Screen — BLOOD): Blood tubes labelled. Lab tech processing type and screen. Blood bank refrigerator in background — units available. She's hemorrhaging. She may need transfusion intra-op. If Rh-negative: RhoGAM indicated. Blood must be available before she goes to OR.

Panel 4 (Pain Control — COMFORT): Nurse injecting IV fentanyl. Patient's face beginning to relax. NPO order posted. No NSAIDs — she's bleeding and NSAIDs impair platelet function. IV opioid: pregnancy-safe for acute surgical pain, fast onset, short duration. Ondansetron drip for nausea — no vomiting into an airway during intubation.

Panel 5 (Surgery — TREATMENT): Operating room. Surgeon, anesthesiologist, nurse. Laparotomy tray open. Lights on. Patient draped. This is what saves her life — not the labs, not the US. The OR. Diagnosis without treatment is futile. Every minute of delay is more blood in the peritoneal cavity.

Panel 6 (Counseling — PREVENTION): Post-op ward. Attending at bedside, speaking gently. Patient listening. "You are not sterile. Infertility means it's harder to conceive — it does not mean impossible. Fertility medications helped you before, but your tubes still work. Every unprotected encounter carries risk." The fundamental misunderstanding that led to this crisis.

Panel 7 (STI Screening — RISK): Lab requisition. Cervical swabs for gonorrhea and chlamydia DNA probe. Chlamydia causes silent salpingitis — tubal scarring without symptoms. Tubal scarring is the number one cause of ectopic pregnancy. She had unprotected sex. She needs screening. Treat if positive — protect the remaining tube.

Panel 8 (Smoking + Alcohol — HABITS): Empty cigarette pack and bottle in trash. Patient looking at them — moment of reflection. Smoking alters tubal motility — ectopic risk factor. Binge drinking led to unprotected sex while intoxicated — the decision she wouldn't have made sober. Both habits contributed to this outcome.

Panel 9 (Discharge — FUTURE): Patient leaving hospital in wheelchair, stable. Discharge papers in hand. Follow-up appointment card. OB-GYN clinic visible. The remaining tube is healthy. She can conceive again, naturally. When she's ready. Protected sex. No alcohol. No cigarettes. The lesson learned.
