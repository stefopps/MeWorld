First Principles · The Mechanism
The trigeminal nerve exits the pons and crosses the prepontine cistern. Right there, the superior cerebellar artery loops over the nerve root entry zone. In 80-90% of cases, this artery compresses the nerve. The compression strips away myelin. Demyelinated axons fire ectopically and form ephaptic connections — a touch signal on one axon jumps across to a pain fiber on its neighbor. A breeze on the cheek becomes lightning in the jaw.

Carbamazepine blocks voltage-gated sodium channels (NaV) — it binds and stabilizes the inactivated state, preventing repetitive firing in demyelinated axons. This is NOT GABAergic. That's gabapentin/pregabalin. Carbamazepine's anti-neuralgic effect is pure sodium channel blockade.

The hyponatremia is an entirely separate mechanism: carbamazepine stimulates ADH release from the posterior pituitary → drug-induced SIADH → water reabsorption in collecting duct → dilutional hyponatremia. This has nothing to do with sodium channels. Two independent effects: NaV blockade (therapeutic) and SIADH (adverse — hence the baseline sodium check).

What I Missed3 diagnostic gaps
▾
CBC with differential
Recent dental procedures. Rule out acute infection before calling it neuralgic. Leukocytosis or bandemia changes the workup from neuro to infectious.
Comprehensive metabolic profile
Carbamazepine blocks sodium channels (NaV) — that's the mechanism. But it also causes SIADH (entirely separate). You need a pre-treatment sodium before starting. If Na drops from 134 to 125, you caught it. No baseline = blind.
CT/MRI head
80-90% are vascular loops. But 10-20% are tumors, MS plaques, or aneurysms. Image to rule out the catastrophic — a cerebellopontine angle tumor presenting as TN is rare but devastating to miss.
The Attending's Lesson
Trigeminal neuralgia is a clinical diagnosis — paroxysmal, electric, seconds-to-minutes, triggered by innocuous stimuli, respecting trigeminal distribution. Exam is normal between attacks. Treatment is carbamazepine. Imaging rules out structural mimics. CBC and CMP are not optional.
