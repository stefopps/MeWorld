Panel 4 (close view, no aspirin): Patient bedside. Aspirin bottle on table with large X mark — not administered. Aspirin would impair platelet function and worsen surgical bleeding. Not ACS — DAPT lethal here.

Panel 5 (wide, OR exterior): View through operating room corridor. CT surgery sign visible. OR lights on. Surgical team scrubbing, gowning. Type A = cardiothoracic surgery. Needs CPB + aortic repair. Never ordered.
